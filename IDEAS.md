# bin2: Invention Ideas

The name has three readings, and every idea below uses at least one:

- **bin to**: turn something into something else.
- **binary**: the raw stuff computers are made of.
- **bin**: let go, throw away, give a second life.

**Chosen:** Idea 1, Files That Never Die. See [docs/ARCHITECTURE.md](docs/ARCHITECTURE.md).

---

## Top 3 picks

### 1. bin2: Files That Never Die (chosen)

**Problem.** Old files stop opening. A 2005 project file, a dead app's format, a game save. Billions of files rot every year.

**Invention.** bin2 wraps any file together with its own viewer. A `.bin2` file opens anywhere, forever: Windows, phone, browser, in 50 years.

**Why people remember it.** Like `.zip`, it becomes a format. "Send it as a bin2 so it always opens."

### 2. bin2: Nutrition Label for Software

**Problem.** You download an `.exe` and have no idea what it will do.

**Invention.** Drop any program on bin2. Before it runs, you get a plain-language label: "Reads your browser data. Connects to 3 servers. Installs at startup. Asks for admin."

**Why people remember it.** Everyone checks the label before running anything, like checking food ingredients.

### 3. bin2: Second Life for Old Devices

**Problem.** Drawers full of old phones and laptops. They work, but nobody uses them.

**Invention.** Install bin2 on any old device and pick a role in one tap: security camera, home server, wall calendar, baby monitor, backup drive, second monitor, Wi-Fi extender.

**Why people remember it.** "Don't throw it away, bin2 it." It saves money and fights e-waste.

## More ideas

### 4. bin2: Move to a New PC in One File

Your whole digital life in one file: installed apps, settings, browser profiles, fonts, Wi-Fi, dev tools. Run it on a new PC and an hour later it feels like your old one.

### 5. bin2: Undo for Your Whole Computer

Like git, but for everything. bin2 quietly records changes to files, settings and installs. You can rewind one thing, like "undo what that installer did yesterday", without restoring a whole backup.

### 6. bin2: Folder to Recipe

Instead of zipping a 3 GB project, bin2 saves a tiny "recipe": the files you wrote, plus how to rebuild everything else. Share 2 MB and the receiver gets the full working folder. This is compression of meaning, not bytes.

### 7. bin2: Which Bin Does This Go In?

Point your phone camera at any piece of waste. bin2 tells you which bin it belongs in, using your city's real rules. If it still has value, it offers it to neighbours or connects you to a recycler who pays.

### 8. bin2: Empty Your Head

Throw any thought into bin2 by voice or text. It sorts each one into **do**, **remember** or **let go**. "Let go" thoughts fade and delete themselves after a few days. Inbox zero for your mind.

### 9. bin2: What Is This File?

Open any unknown file and bin2 explains it in plain words: what it is, what made it, what's hidden inside, and how to open or convert it.

### 10. bin2: Offline Pass-the-File

Two phones and no internet. bin2 sends files by sound, QR bursts or Bluetooth mesh between nearby phones. Built for villages, disasters, exams and flights.
